The Program

Two days, mile by mile

Day 1 covers the current state of AI implementation — safety and system performance. Day 2 shifts to maintenance, longevity, and the strategic role of AI in long-term planning.

Day 1 focuses on the current state of AI implementation, addressing critical areas like road safety and optimizing system performance (ITS and mobility). MC: Pam Cotter and/or Dan DiBiasio.

Day 2 shifts focus to the maintenance, longevity, and future development of road and mobility assets, and the strategic role of AI in long-term planning. MC: Pam Cotter and/or Dan DiBiasio.
