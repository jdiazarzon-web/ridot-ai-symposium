Thomas Grünstäudl is Head of Project Development at ASFINAG BMG, where he is responsible for the development of major motorway and expressway infrastructure projects in Austria.

He earned a Master’s Degree in Environmental Engineering from the University of Natural Resources and Life Sciences, Vienna (BOKU). Following several years in civil engineering with a focus on rail and road projects, he joined ASFINAG as a Project Manager and has led the Project Development Department since 2013.
