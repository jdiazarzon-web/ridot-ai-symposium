Derek has served as the Rhode Island DA since December 2022, leading a multi-disciplinary team to deliver a nearly $600 million Federal-aid Highway Program in Rhode Island, in partnership with the Rhode Island Department of Transportation and other stakeholders. Prior to this position, Derek served as a Special Assistant in the Office of the Administrator where he provided direct support to the Administrator for agency operations, execution of leadership priorities, and coordination with the Office of the Secretary.  Derek also served as a legislative and policy analyst in the Office of Policy and Governmental Affairs. Before joining FHWA, Derek served for eight years as a policy advisor for members of the United States House of Representatives, where his portfolio included transportation, agriculture, environment, and energy issues.
