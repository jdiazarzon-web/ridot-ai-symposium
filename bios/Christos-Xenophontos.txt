Christos S. XENOPHONTOS has over 40 years of experience in the transportation field as an engineer, project manager, and administrator, mostly with RIDOT. He currently leads the agency’s Research & Innovation and climate change initiatives. He serves as Chair of PIARC’s (World Road Association) TC 1.1, Performance of Transport Administrations, Chair of AASHTO’s Committee on Performance Based Management, and co-vice Chair of TRB’s Executive Subcommittee on International Activities (E0004).
Christos is passionate about innovation, organizational excellence, and public value creation, and the integration of emerging technologies in transportation agencies. He is dedicated to advancing agencies from infrastructure-centric to customer-centered organizations that prioritize societal benefits. He has been a champion of knowledge transfer and believes that through the convening of technology transfer activities we can find better solutions to the transportation challenges we are facing.
