Jinhua Zhao is Professor, and Department head at MIT. He is one of the most sought-after authorities on urban mobility and AI-integrated transportation systems. He directs the JTL Urban Mobility Lab, the field's leading research lab. He reshapes traditional engineering equations of transportation by integrating behavioral science with computation intelligence to build grounded AI that achieve superior operation optimization, rider experience, and workforce productivity. For two decades, Jinhua bridges groundbreaking research with high-impact advisory work for governments, transportation agencies, and corporations across the globe in London, Chicago, Washington DC, Boston, Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan and Saudi Arabia.
