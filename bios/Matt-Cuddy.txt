Matt Cuddy focuses on AI strategy at the Volpe National Transportation Systems Center. Since 2022, he has led Volpe's support of the Intelligent Transportation Systems Joint Program Office's AI Program and managed the USDOT AI Community of Practice. His earlier work includes leading Volpe’s support to both the ITS JPO’s initiative on smart cities and the USDOT Technology Transfer Program, coordinating research at Northwestern University’s Transportation Center, developing urban planning standards for the State of New Jersey, and developing hybrid vehicle drivetrains at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (then known as the National Renewable Energy Laboratory) . He has degrees in mechanical engineering from Cornell and the University of Colorado, Boulder, and a Ph.D. in urban policy development from Rutgers.
