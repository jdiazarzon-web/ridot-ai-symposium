Dr. Timothy Henry brings over 40 years of experience in computer science and information systems across both industry and academia. In industry, he managed large-scale projects and led teams developing complex information systems, including statewide child welfare systems, healthcare systems, and mobile projects supporting first responders. His expertise in strategic planning, budgeting, and system design consistently drove successful outcomes.

His academic career includes faculty roles at multiple universities. He currently serves as Chair of the Computer Science and Information Systems Department at Rhode Island College and Interim Director of Rhode Island's AI Hub, and has directed cybersecurity centers. An accomplished author, he has co-authored award-winning textbooks and served on numerous boards and committees, advancing community health and education while contributing to state and national efforts to shape standards for AI, cybersecurity, and computer science education.
