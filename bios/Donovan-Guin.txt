Based in Massachusetts, Donovan Guin oversees IBM's transportation practice in the United States for state and local government, focused on Public Transit, DOTs, DMVs, Tolling, Airports and Ports. His career has followed the arc of the digital revolution, helping commercial clients like Walmart, Best Buy and Johnson & Johnson to adapt to a digital-first economy. Over the last decade, he has brought this know-how to public agencies, helping communities better adapt to the changing patterns of driving and ridership due to digital commerce, remote work and artificial intelligence. A frequent speaker and guest lecturer, Donovan holds a Master of Science in Technology Leadership from Brown University.
