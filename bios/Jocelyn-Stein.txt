Jocelyn Stein leads the Business Integration & Digital Accessibility section at the Minnesota Department of Transportation. With nearly three decades of experience in Minnesota state government, Jocelyn brings a people‑centered, empathy‑driven approach to business technology, ensuring systems and processes genuinely support the individuals and business outcomes they are designed to enable. Her work focuses on aligning digital accessibility, business integration, and responsible innovation, including emerging applications of generative AI, to the operational needs and strategic priorities of the agency. Jocelyn holds PMP and CSPO certifications and is known for fostering collaborative partnerships and delivering practical, equitable solutions that add value across the organization.
