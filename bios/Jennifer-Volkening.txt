Jennifer Volkening serves as the Director of the Data Analytics and Governance Division at the Utah Department of Transportation (UDOT), where she leads agency-wide efforts to modernize legacy systems, establish strong data governance and management frameworks, and guide UDOT's strategic AI adoption. Joining in 2024, she brings deep industry experience in data architecture, engineering, and enterprise analytics. Her work ensures seamless data sharing across divisions to support performance-based planning and data-driven decision-making statewide. Jennifer also represents the department on multi-state and national committees for responsible AI adoption and implementation. Based in Salt Lake City, she enjoys skiing, hiking, camping, and bird watching.
