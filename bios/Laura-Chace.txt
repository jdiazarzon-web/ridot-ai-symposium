Laura Chace was named President and CEO of ITS America in August 2021. Her focus is to champion policies and investments that make our transportation systems safer, more innovative, and more efficient. Working collaboratively with agencies and industry leaders, Chace advances technology-driven solutions that improve how people and goods move – enhancing quality of life, supporting a thriving economy, and maintaining America’s global competitiveness.

Chace has served on several advisory committees to U.S. DOT regarding the integration of emerging technologies into our transportation system and is currently serving on the Advisory Board of the National Center for Infrastructure Transformation, the Advisory Board for International Association of Transportation Regulators, and the Steering Committee for the Accelerator for America’s Innovative Infrastructure Initiative (i3) group.
