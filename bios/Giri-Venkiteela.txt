Giri Venkiteela serves as a Research Scientist at NJDOT's Bureau of Research and Innovation, where he leads research and innovation initiatives including the NJDOT Innovation Program, Artificial Intelligence Program, NJSTIC, and Transportation Pooled-Fund programs. He chairs the AASHTO-RAC Region #1 and Technologies Taskforce and actively contributes to NCHRP projects and TRB/AASHTO committees. Dr. Venkiteela has presented NJDOT research at national and international conferences (TRB, AASHTO, IRF) and reviews research proposals and journals for various agencies and contribute as an author for many journals. He holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the University of Louisville and has conducted research at Northwestern and Rutgers Universities.
