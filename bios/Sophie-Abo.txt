As a transportation planner at the U.S. DOT Volpe Center, Sophie’s focus includes integrating emerging technologies and data into transportation planning and operations. Abo works with a range of agencies, including the Federal Transit Administration, the Intelligent Transportation Systems Joint Program Office, the National Park Service, and the U.S. Forest Service. Prior to joining the U.S. DOT Volpe Center, Abo worked at the Brookings Institution's Metro Program, and she managed the transportation demand management program at a private real-estate developer in Pittsburgh, PA. She holds a MA of Public Policy and Management from Carnegie Mellon University, and a BA in Anthropology: Global Health and the Environment from Washington University in St. Louis.
