Arnulf Hagen is the CEO and Founder of Aidalos, a Norwegian AI company developing cognitive multi-agent systems for complex engineering and infrastructure applications. He holds various degrees, including a PhD from NTNU and an MSc from MIT, and brings over 30 years of experience spanning structural engineering, digital twins, and applied AI — including prior roles as Chief Scientist at SAP and founder/CEO of two companies successfully acquired by DNV and SAP respectively. At this webinar, Arnulf will present on combining AI agents with digital twins for asset health assessment in transportation infrastructure.
