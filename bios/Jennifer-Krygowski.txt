Jennifer Hall is the deputy director-chief of staff for the American Association of State Highway and Transportation Officials (AASHTO), overseeing the engineering, safety and mobility, publications, and meetings/member services divisions at AASHTO. In this role, she works with the respective directors and team members of those divisions to provide leadership and guidance for the work of AASHTO's committees, the production of the association's technical standards and publications, and the coordination of more than three dozen industry meetings and conferences. In addition to those duties, she serves as AASHTO's general counsel and oversees the association's legal affairs, including the contracts and agreements AASHTO enters into with outside organizations, state agencies, and the federal government.
