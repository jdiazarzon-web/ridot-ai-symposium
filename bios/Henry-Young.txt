Dr. Henry Young is Assistant Provost at the New England Institute of Technology, administering the Engineering, Construction, and Architecture programs. He also serves as university liaison to international institutions, fostering global partnerships and collaboration. Before joining New England Tech, Dr. Young served as an officer in the U.S. Navy Civil Engineer Corps. He later held positions as Project Engineer and Special Assistant to the Director at RIDOT, before joining KPMG Peat Marwick as a Senior Consultant specializing in strategic planning. In 1998, he founded Young & Young Management Consultants, providing project management and planning services to governmental organizations. Dr. Young holds a Doctor of Education from Northeastern University, a Master of Science in Civil Engineering from the University of Rhode Island, and a Bachelor of Science in Civil Engineering from Virginia Tech. He has also completed executive studies at the London School of Economics.
